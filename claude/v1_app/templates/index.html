What it does

A factorized CNN encoder (Klindt-style) is trained per recording session on a single Ringach-lab pepANA file. It predicts a 16-channel firing-rate time series at ~30 Hz from the stimulus history of the past ~270 ms, with a 60 ms response latency.

This is a proof of concept. Predictions reflect what the model learned about these 16 electrodes from one penetration in one animal, not visual cortex in general.
